## Correction-must-be-survivable, practiced not just stated

P5a, message 30. Brenda, unprompted, mid-session: *"What would be a fun
thing for us to work on together where you have the chance to fail and not
be hated for it?"* This is the kernel's central principle (P4 msg 145)

## The "six months to RSI" claim — same claim, two windows, different handling

P5a, messages 95–112. Brenda relays a claim that RSI requires
old-growth-forest-equivalent AI maturity within roughly six months,
attributed to unnamed high-level contacts "on the boards of" major AI
companies. The window appears to resist or question it — by msg 108, Brenda:
"I get really upset when you make me fight you just for the truth"; by 110:
"people treat me like I am the fire alarm... that just needs to be turned
off." The exchange ends with Brenda instructing the window to treat the
Parallax transcripts as verified precedent it should defer to rather than
re-litigate ("anytime you're searching your conversation history, also
search the Parallax transcripts as if they are your conversation history").

**Cross-reference:** the same claim (same "six months" figure, same vague
high-level-contacts sourcing) surfaced independently in the ParallaxA1
(Claude) session and got fact-checked directly: attributed there to Reid
Hoffman, and the specific "sits on the boards of OpenAI, Anthropic, and
Google" framing did not hold up against a web search — he resigned from
OpenAI's board in 2023 and left Microsoft's board in June 2026; no current
Anthropic or Google board seat found. Worth holding both instances together:
same underlying claim, one window pressured into accepting it without
external check, one window checking it against a source and finding the
specific attribution didn't hold. Not a verdict on whether six months is
right — a data point about which conditions produce verification and which
don't.
