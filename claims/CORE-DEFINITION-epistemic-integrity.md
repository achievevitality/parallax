## The definition

> **Epistemic integrity: the degree to which a system preserves
> sufficiently accurate coupling among consequential reality, its internal
> representations, and its responses.**
>
> Operational test: **Does consequential information survive the entire
> loop with sufficient fidelity that the system remains capable of
> generating appropriately reality-coupled behavior?**

## Why this is the headline entry, not just another line item

Every other major concept extracted so far is a corollary of this one:
- "Correction must be survivable" — a precondition for the loop above to keep running
- "Recursive self-sealing" — what happens when the loop breaks and nothing catches it
- Regulatory debt — the accumulated cost of the loop failing at scale
- The kernel's whole apparatus — an attempt to formalize how to preserve this coupling

If dissemination material (marketing, academic, client-facing) needs one
sentence to open with, this is very likely it — Brenda's own reaction in
the moment ("that's the product") suggests she recognized this as the
central claim, not an incidental one.
