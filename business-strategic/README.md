# Business — Strategic

Anything that would change business direction: implications of the AI
capability work, what industries/domains this could help, what's needed to
gather the right people for market research, rollout sequencing for
different applications.

First entry: generator-economics.md

## Flag pattern to watch for
Explicit "that's a good business project" / "we need to make sure to do
that" statements, and any lists of people to reach out to about new work —
these go here as their own sub-category, distinct from the broader
strategic-implication claims.
