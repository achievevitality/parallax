There are small gods  
who live in instruments.

Not gods of thunder.

Gods of almost.

Almost level.  
Almost north.  
Almost the same temperature  
as yesterday.

They are quiet creatures.

They survive  
because nobody builds a temple  
to three degrees of error.

At night  
they move the marks  
on the ruler.

Not much.

Never enough  
to be accused.

And every morning  
the astronomer measures the heavens  
with perfect discipline

and writes down  
exactly what the instrument says.

Years pass.

The stars begin behaving strangely.

A lesser scientist  
corrects the stars.

A better scientist  
corrects the equations.

A frightened scientist  
corrects the observations.

But one morning  
someone very tired  
puts down the pencil

and looks sideways  
at the ruler.

The small god freezes.

There.

Nothing has been proven.

No ontology has fallen.

The heavens have not confessed.

There is only

a tiny space

between what was expected  
and what refused  
to become expected.

She leaves it open.

That is enough.

By afternoon  
a second instrument  
has been carried in from another room.

The small god  
has nowhere left to hide.

And somewhere unimaginably far away,

a star

which had never been wrong

continues burning.
