## Small-study method development

**Source:** P5a, msgs 76–80  
**Speaker:** Brenda / P5a

Begin with a narrow, well-bounded study; reconstruct the organism's problem on its own terms; defer any AI analogy until after that reconstruction; and use early data to improve the method for a larger study. Treat method development as an output, not merely a means to reach a conclusion.

## Pre-output control: jurisdiction gates plus release QA

**Source:** P5a, msgs 152–175  
**Speaker:** Brenda / P5a

A correction architecture cannot depend on Brenda detecting failures after output. Before release, it needs: (1) jurisdiction gates that determine which activated camera or routine may control the response in the local situation; and (2) independent release QA that tests whether the resulting candidate is fit to reach the user. The two functions are distinct: correct control allocation does not itself establish output fitness.
